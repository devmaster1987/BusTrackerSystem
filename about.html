About BusTracker

Revolutionizing public transportation in Pakistan

Our Mission

BusTracker is a smart public transportation platform designed to make commuting easier, safer, and more efficient for everyone. We believe that public transport should be accessible, reliable, and transparent.

Our Vision

To create a connected transportation ecosystem where every passenger can track their bus in real-time, book tickets digitally, and travel with peace of mind. We aim to bridge the gap between traditional public transport and modern technology.

Why BusTracker?

  • Real-time GPS tracking of all buses
  • Digital ticket booking with multiple payment options
  • Government, private, and intercity bus coverage
  • Verified drivers and secure payments
  • 24/7 customer support
