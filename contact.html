Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about BusTracker

How do I book a ticket?

Simply open the Passenger App, search for your desired bus, select it, choose your payment method, and confirm your booking. You'll receive a QR code ticket instantly.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept JazzCash, Easypaisa, Credit/Debit Cards, Wallet Balance, and Cash Payment options for your convenience.

Is real-time tracking available?

Yes! All buses are equipped with GPS tracking. You can see the live location of your bus on the map in real-time.

How do I become a driver?

Contact our driver recruitment team through the contact form or email us at drivers@bustracker.com. We'll guide you through the verification and onboarding process.

Is my payment secure?

Absolutely! We use industry-standard encryption and secure payment gateways to protect all your financial transactions.

Can I cancel my ticket?

Yes, you can cancel your ticket up to 15 minutes before the scheduled departure time. A cancellation fee may apply depending on the bus service.

Need Help?

Our support team is here to assist you 24/7.
